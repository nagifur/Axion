X-415 is seperated into two beings, as they seem to differentiate from one another. There is limited information, only such gathered from drawings and audio recordings.

X-415-1 is classified as a variant human, with nothing but an eye for his facial features. He seems to be human otherwise, acting as such with pristine survival levels of intelligence. It seems to warn others of itself, seeming to fear X-415-2 when it “grows hungry” or “senses territory dispute”.

X-415-2 Is the main concern, and main reason this being still roams Frostpoint. It is a hulking beast of a conglomerate hive mind; the beings speaking individually, yet all at once. They tend to sprout from X-415-1 during hostile environments or territorial disputes. Notably, the incident of Site X-0209 is the reason why this being is considered dangerous, wiping out many of our security team over the span of a few days, leaving only one survivor. 

In death, they seem to reanimate corpses of those who they do not devour. It is currently unknown the methods to their selection of who is reanimated or who is not. The process seemingly entails hollowing the victim’s body out and allowing voidals to take place of the vital organs, returning bloodflow to the brain and “Swarming” their body, allowing cognitive control of all physical movement. Some security units showed signs of mental awareness, brain scans indicating erratic readings of fear and overwhelming anxiety before extermination.

We will continue to document encounters, as Frostpoint has many valuable caverns full of crystals imbued with magical properties we wish to investigate.
