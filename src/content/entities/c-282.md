Tall lizard-like creature with exceptional hearing and an unknown language capability. They seem to have a heightened sense of awareness despite their lack of eyesight.

On their home planet they appear to live in tribal communities, relying heavily on their acute hearing to communicate and navigate their environment despite their lack of vision. They have developed basic language skills that are primarily auditory, using a combination of clicks, whistles, and other sounds to convey information and maintain social bonds within their communities.

Writing has been found carved into stone surfaces, often consisting of simple symbols and patterns that likely represent their auditory language.
