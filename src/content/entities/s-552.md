Tall herbivorous dinosaur known for its docile nature. Lives in a controlled enclosure within the facility. 

 Found on ████████ Sprouty primarily feeds on plant matter and poses little threat to humans under normal circumstances. After a meal it tends to rest and digest quietly, showing little activity until it is ready to feed again, which can take a week or more depending on the size of the meal. 

While it only eats plant matter, it seems capable of digesting some meat if swallowed accidentally. In those cases it seems to process the meat without any noticeable issues, although it does not actively seek out or consume meat as part of its diet, and it does make it hibernate for extended periods after large meals.

Remains of an intern were found in its ████, suggesting that it may have accidentally ingested the individual. As to why this occurred, it remains unclear, but it is likely an unintended consequence of its feeding behavior. Intern must have wandered into its enclosure at an inopportune moment and been consumed accidentally, after a week or more of digestion the remains were expelled and discovered by the facility staff.

While minimal care is required due to its docile nature, the facility caretakes ensure it has a consistent supply of fresh plants from its home environment as well as cleaning and maintaining its enclosure regularly to provide a safe and comfortable living space.

This creature seems to be quite rare, as we have not found any more of its species on its home planet ████████ despite extensive surveys and exploration efforts.
