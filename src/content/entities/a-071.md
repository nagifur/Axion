### Initial Contact

Found on Thalassar, a planet known for its extreme oceanic conditions and deep-sea ecosystems. Levi was initially discovered by a research team studying the planet's unique marine life. A large leviathan creature, it immediately drew attention due to its size and unusual features. Despite the harsh environment, Levi demonstrated remarkable intelligence and adaptability. While it seemed initially cautious, Levi quickly established a bond with the researchers, who recognized its potential as an ally and worked to gain its trust.
The creature was tamed by axion to help as a transport into the deepest parts of the ocean. It can adjust its skin and internal organs to adapt to change in water pressure and acidity. 

### Behavior and Abilities

While vocal communication is not possible, Levi exhibits a high level of intelligence and problem-solving skills, often anticipating the needs of the research team. He communicates using a combination of body language, bioluminescent signals, and subtle changes in skin texture to convey emotions and intentions. 

His body is exceptionally well-adapted to the extreme pressures and conditions of the acid ocean. And has been observed to dive up to 60 000 meters below the surface without sustaining any injuries. This remarkable adaptation allows Levi to serve as a reliable transport mechanism for deep-sea exploration, capable of withstanding environments that would be fatal to most other organisms or vehicles.

Levi's main ability lies in his capacity to transport personnel and materials inside his body, making him an invaluable asset for deep-sea exploration and research. Levi swallows personnel and equipment and adjusts its stomach acidity and internal pressure to ensure their safety and comfort during transport. They seem to have fully adapted to this role, showing remarkable patience and cooperation throughout the process.

It is possible to enter Levi's body through other entrances such as the anus or penis, both of which can be used for transport as they are safe from the water pressure. Prefered method of entry is through the mouth, as it provides the most easy access, but the choice is ultimately up to the personnel being transported.

> Chosing the penis as the method of transport will take a bit longer during exit as compared to the mouth due to Levi needing to ejaculate in order to safely expel the transported personnel.

### Training and Reinforcement

Levi is rewarded with food after each successful transport mission, reinforcing positive behavior and ensuring continued cooperation with the research team. There are no accidents on record for the last several months where Levi would fail to safely transport personnel or equipment. The only incidents have been during training where Levi would mistake personnel for food rewards, leading to digestion of the mistaken personnel. These incidents have decreased significantly as Levi has become more accustomed to distinguishing between training exercises and actual transport missions.
