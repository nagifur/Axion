Magical colors that bring anything drawn with them to life.  When drawn on a canvas or wall, it becomes animated and can interact with the environment as if it were real, a living painting capable of independent movement and actions. When drawn in mid-air, the creations take on a three-dimensional form and can move freely within the space although they will eventually disappear.

The ammount of paint seems to regenerate over time, ensuring that the artifact remains usable even after extensive use, the speed of regeneration depends on magic particle density and ambient magical energy.

The artifact itself appears as a set of vibrant, multicolored paints contained in a small, round containers. 

> This artifact is currently in the possession of Canvas, a former level 3 caretaker who stole it from its containment.
