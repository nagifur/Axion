Carnivorous plant with tentacle-like appendages that can trap prey. Its body is made up of thick, flexible green tendrils, broad fleshy leaves, and bulbous plant structures. The surface is smooth, glossy, and coated in a thick layer of sticky mucus.

Entity is actively predatory, using its tentacle-like appendages to ensnare and immobilize prey before consuming it. It's tendrils can reach out and grab prey from a distance, and pushing it inside its bulbous structure, where digestion takes place.

Once prey is squeezed through a flexible opening into its bulbous structure, the plant secretes digestive enzymes to break down the captured organism, absorbing nutrients over time. The opening then closes tightly, preventing escape and ensuring the prey is fully digested.

It seems to usually capture small animals, but larger prey may also be targeted if it can be overpowered and pulled into its bulbous structure. As seen by the discovery of an intern's skull inside, it is capable of handling relatively large prey. An unaware intern most likely got caught by its tentacles and pulled inside, where he was subsequently digested over time.

Thrives in humid, tropical environments with ample sunlight and nutrient-rich soil, where it can effectively capture and digest prey to sustain its growth.
