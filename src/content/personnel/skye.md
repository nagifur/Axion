Skye is a level 4 elite robotics expert that was hired by Axion after seeing their talent while they were still studying. They accepted our invitation to join our team, and have been working with us ever since. Skye is known for their superb understanding of robotics and their Artificial Intelligence, which makes them a valuable asset to our team.

Skye is a talented prodigy that has been working with robotics since they were a child. They have a natural talent for understanding how things work, and have been able to create some of the most advanced robots we have available.

They seem to have an obsession with the mech FF2400, affectionately called "Scarlet" which they have been known to spend months working on, after finishing their work on the mech, which was blueprinted by Dr. Mages.
