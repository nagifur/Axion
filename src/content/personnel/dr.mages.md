Dr. Mages is the lead scientist and head of the R&D department at the facility. They are one of the founding members of Axion, and have been working with the organization since its inception along with Sypher.

Mages is in possesion of an extremely powerful core artifact called "Heart of the Archmage" which is a powerful artifact that allows them to freely use any magic without needing to spend years of training. The only thing they need is to learn the incantations and the spell will be casted. This artifact is extremely powerful, and is one of the reasons why Mages is able to create such advanced technology.

Majority of tech at Axion has been designed by Mages, they love adding their own "quirks" that tend to be more on the sexual side, such as the "cum disposal unit" which is a device that is able to dispose of any unwanted personell, and is often used by A1 to dispose of bad employees.

Dr.Mages is often a target of Axion's enemies, and they have tried to kidnap them multiple times, but have always failed due to the security measures in place at the facility.
