Mellisa is the head of the Biocare department at Axion, she is responsible for the production of the best healing and medical tools available. She produces healing honey from her breasts and stomach which is used to heal any injuries or even regrow lost limbs.

Found on planet ████, Mellisa was found weak, in an destroyed Hive, unable to survive on her own without the help of her hive. She was taken in by Axion, and has been working with them ever since. Axion provided Mellisa a section of the facility on level 4 that was converted into a hive.

Mellisa needs her workers to feed her, she has her own hive of workers that are responsible for feeding her and taking care of her needs. Mellisa can be a very seductive and hard to resist, as her pheromones cause mind control.
