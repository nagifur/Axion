Wisp was a former level 3 employee at Axion, during a containment breach Wisp was attacked by ███████ where her chest was crushed as her dying body was thrown by the creature into S-013, in which her soul fused with her body, shattering the artifact in the proccess. ,after "dying" and gaining new form Wisp was promoted to level 5 due to her new abilities.

Even after "death" Wisp continues to be highly religious, and swears she can see the souls of the dead, converse with them , and even able to see "the other side", as to what that exactly means remains unknown.

Her ghost form allows her to be fully immortal, as she does not appear to age, not get injured by any kind of attack. Which comes in very handy during visits of dangerous contained entities.

While Wisp usually has no corporeal form she can manifest a physical form temporarily, which she uses to interact with the world around her. She is also able to phase through walls and other solid objects, making her a valuable asset in containment situations.
