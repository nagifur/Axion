Ahti “Aker” Nuria is an AEGIS Security operative currently assigned to Axion through the standing cooperative personnel agreement between both organizations. Within AEGIS, Nuria serves as a weapons specialist attached to Grayline, a specialized security unit operating under the direct authority of Security Director Phelix Adler.

Standing 7'2" and possessing considerable natural strength in addition to extensive combat training, Nuria is primarily utilized by Axion as a Heavy Containment Specialist. His duties include assisting with the containment, transport, restraint, and emergency management of entities or specimens considered unsafe for conventional personnel to handle.

Despite his imposing appearance and combat-oriented background, Nuria is generally cooperative, approachable, and protective of personnel working alongside him. He displays a strong preference for placing himself between potential threats and less-protected personnel, occasionally to a degree considered unnecessarily hazardous to himself.

Nuria possesses extensive experience with firearms, heavy weapons, hostile environments, and unconventional threats. His equipment includes a personally fitted protective armor system designed to accommodate both terrestrial and aquatic operations. While capable of functioning independently, he demonstrates particularly strong performance when given a clearly defined objective and freedom to adapt his methods to changing conditions.

Personnel should be aware that Nuria's physiology and place of origin differ from species native to Axion's primary operational environment. Medical treatment, equipment modification, and biological assessment should therefore reference the documentation supplied by AEGIS rather than relying exclusively on local species standards.

Nuria has previously been exposed to anomalous phenomena during AEGIS operations, including Artifact 138. Relevant medical and psychological documentation concerning this exposure is restricted and available to authorized personnel where operationally necessary. The exposure does not currently prohibit him from performing assigned duties.

Despite his temporary assignment, Nuria remains an employee of AEGIS and is subject to AEGIS recall, operational restrictions, and applicable inter-organizational agreements.
