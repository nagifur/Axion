Subject suffers from a condition limiting her memory retention to a maximum span of exactly two (2) months.

Accommodations: To counteract this, the subject is permitted to maintain an extensive physical archive—including notes, daily logs, and evaluation transcripts—within her personal quarters. Instinctual Blood Tracking: While unconscious, the subject actively relies on primal olfactory senses to detect and track the source of blood within the facility.

Auditory Recognition / Partial Consciousness: A fraction of the subject's consciousness remains active during these episodes. She can hear and recognize familiar voices and identities of personnel, though her physical body's compliance with verbal commands is entirely unpredictable.

Pacification Protocol: Despite the predatory nature of this state, the subject is highly susceptible to physical pacification. Specifically, scratching underneath her chin has proven to successfully subdue and pacify her.
