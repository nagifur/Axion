Somni is a level 3 containment unit specialist at Axion, known for her expertise in handling any threat and all kinds of alien creatures.

Born on the planet, ████, Somni decided to join Axion after her desire to get away from her home planet. After a background check with her government, it seemed she was an outcast among her people. She was then recruited by Axion, and has been working with them ever since.

Somni is in possession of highly powerful <b>ESP</b> abilities, which she uses to her advantage in her work. It is a new type of magic, that is not present on Axion's home planet. She is also a skilled combatant, and is able to take down any threat that comes her way. Due to her powers she was able to ace all of our combat training, and is now one of our top containment agents.
>There seems to be something more inside her, reading up on local culture on planet ████, apparently she is in possesion of some demonic powers, if this is just a myth or a real thing needs to be tested.

She seems to have a liking for the caretaker, Nagi, and is often seen spending time with him during her breaks.
