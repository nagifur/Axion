Former hero of the ocean on planet Azuron, Reef was recruited by Axion after he was found <b>mutated</b> by a core artifact called the "Fang of the Abyss" He was uncontrollable and was causing havoc on his home planet, so he was taken in by Axion to be studied and to help him control his powers.

Axion managed to partially revert the transformation, thanks to ███████ █████, after which Reef was able to control himself again, and use the power of the artifact to his advangtage. Reef is now the head of security at the facility, and is responsible for keeping the facility safe from any threats and intruders.

He always wears the artifact around his neck, and gets withdrawals if hes seperated from it for too long. The artifact seems to influence his mind, and he needs to eat for two, to keep the artifact fed too, as it appears its some kind of living entity in itself.
