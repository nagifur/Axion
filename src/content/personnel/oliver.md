Project name: "Homunculus". A biological synthetic. Part of the Axion test trials in the biological lab development. Axion's first successful test subject, Oliver, was raised and educated in Axion. Despite lacking desirable personality traits, Oliver possessed telepathic abilities to communicate messages across broad ranges and was able to understand different life forms through this mental connection. After exhausting current studies, he has been placed as Nagi's personal assistant due to Nagi's charismatic personality as the Caretaker. Oliver's skills and abilities also best support Nagi in his work and help him stay alive through various situations. Axion still monitors Oliver to this day.
