## Origin Planet

Axion was founded on its home planet of Ventria 16 years ago by our founder Sypher, and Dr. Mages. What once used to be a planet full of magic and nature, has now been transformed into one of the most technologically advanced planets in the known galaxy. 

### Ventria

- Equatorial diameter: 14,670 km
- Mass: 7.89 × 10^24 kg
- Surface gravity: 9.81 m/s²
- Density: 4.79 g/cm³
- Orbital period: 365 days
- Atmosphere: Nitrogen, Oxygen, Argon, Carbon Dioxide

> Warning: Current atmosphere is quickly deteriorating due to the fast expansion and industrialization of the planet by Axion. 
    
## Magic

Magic is something that everyone can use in the universe. If a person is passionate enough about their craft, they can learn to use magic. 
For example if a person studies enough of biology, they can learn to use magic to heal others or manipulate the body. If you study nature, plants, animals, you can learn nature magic, such as in case of Nagi. 

Anyone can learn any type of magic, but it takes a lot of time and dedication to master it. Some people are born with a natural talent for magic, while others have to work hard to learn it.

## Public Reaction to Axion

Axion is a controversial company, and has been the subject of much debate and criticism. Although lately the public perception is shifting, and more people are starting to see the benefits of Axion's technology.

From public view, Axion is a company that is trying to improve the world by technology, and leave magic behind. However, there are small groups of people who believe that Axion is a company that is trying to take over the world. 

Goverments are too afraid to speak out againts Axion, as they are too powerful and influential.
