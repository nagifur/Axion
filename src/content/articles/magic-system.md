## Overview

Magic exists everywhere in the universe. It is a force that has existed since the beginning of time, influencing the natural order and the development of the universe. Axion was the first organization to systematically study and understand magic.

Magical particles (p) flow through the universe, in something known as the Magical Field (Mf). Those particles attach to living beings, objects, and even the environment, enabling the manipulation and utilization of magic.

## Magical Field - Mf

An invisible field that permeates the entire universe, the Magical Field (Mf) serves as the medium through which magical particles (p) travel and interact with the world. It is the foundation of all magical phenomena, and its properties determine how magic can be harnessed and manipulated. While this field can vary in density (ρM), it is generally stable and omnipresent, ensuring that magic is always accessible throughout the universe. Places without any magic particles (p) are extremely rare and are considered voids where magical influence is negligible or entirely absent. It is mostly caused by devices/structures that manipulate or absorb magical paricles (p), creating localized areas with reduced magical presence.
 
### Example
Particle density on an open field would be such as 

ρM ≈ 1.2×10^8 p/m^3.
