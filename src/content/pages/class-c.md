---
title: Containment
---

Class C is the default classification that is considered when first evaluating an entity's containment requirements. Before an entity is assigned to a higher or lower class, it is typically placed under Class C containment to ensure that standard safety protocols are followed. This classification indicates that the entity requires dedicated containment measures and careful handling procedures to prevent risk to personnel or the facility.

### Attributes
Entities of Class C typically exhibit the following attributes:
- Moderate threat level
- Requires dedicated containment measures
- Careful handling procedures
- Standard safety protocols must be followed

### Rights

- Access to appropriate containment facilities
- Interaction with personnel under controlled conditions
- Movement within designated containment areas

### Containment

Class C entities are typically contained at Level 2 or Level 3 in specialized containment facilities. These facilities are designed to provide secure and controlled environments to minimize the risk posed by the entity. Personnel must adhere to strict safety protocols when interacting with Class C entities, and containment areas are regularly monitored to ensure compliance with safety standards.
