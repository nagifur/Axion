	<h3 style="margin:0;">Light Containment</h3>

</div>


Light containment is where most of S class entities are kept with some C class ones as well. Entities contained here need little to no interaction from personnel, as they are generally stable and low-risk under standard containment protocols. 

Most cells are locked with metal doors and reinforced with security systems to prevent unauthorized access. No other special containment measures are typically required for entities housed in this level.

Main monitoring station for whole of Axion, overseeing all sectors and ensuring the security and proper functioning of the facility.

While A1 has a direct feed and monitoring for all cameras throughout the facility, personnel are still required to perform regular camera checks and inspections to ensure everything is functioning correctly.

In-case A1 is not available, and facility is in manual operation mode, personnel must monitor the facility themselves and report any irregularities to the nearest security or supervisory personnel.

Monitoring sector has a direct line of communication with security sector on Level 3.

	<h3 style="margin:0;">Maintenance</h3>
	<img src="/icons/maintenance.svg" alt="Maintenance icon" width="50" height="50" style="display:block; margin: 0 0 0 0;" />
</div>

Maintenance sector is responsible for the upkeep and repair of all facility infrastructure, including electrical systems, plumbing, and mechanical equipment. Personnel assigned to this sector ensure that all systems are functioning optimally and address any issues that arise promptly.

Even though its only located on Level 2, the maintenance sector plays a crucial role in ensuring the smooth operation of the entire facility.

Personnel from maintenance often get temporary access to other sectors when performing repairs or inspections, ensuring that all areas of the facility remain operational and safe.
