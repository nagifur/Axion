---
title: Maximum Containment
---

Entities classified as Class M are considered extremely dangerous and require maximum security containment protocols at all times. Most interactions with these entities are highly restricted and closely monitored to prevent any potential breaches or catastrophic incidents. A breach in containment can have severe consequences, potentially endangering the entire facility or even the whole world.

### Attributes

Entities of Class M typically exhibit the following attributes:
- Extremely high threat level
- Requires maximum security containment measures
- Strict handling procedures
- Continuous monitoring and surveillance
- Limited interaction with personnel
- Specialized containment facilities
- High risk of catastrophic consequences if containment fails
- Requires specialized emergency response protocols

### Rights

- Confined to specialized containment cell at all times

### Containment

Class M entities are contained in the highest security facilities available at Level 3 heavy containment. Most entities require specific containment protocols tailored to their unique threat profiles, including reinforced containment cells, freezing or hot chambers, and specialized restraint systems.

> Personnel interacting with Class M entities without proper adherence to containment protocols are very often consumed by the entities themselves, leading to death. In lot of cases it may not even be possible to recover the remains, emphasizing the critical importance of strict compliance with containment procedures.
