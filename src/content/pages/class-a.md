---
title: Ally
---

Entities classified as Class A are considered friendly, cooperative, and non-hostile towards Axion personnel. They may assist with research, or otherwise coexist peacefully within the facility. A creature with the class A is considered a full fledged employee of the facility and gets the same rights and privileges as any other staff member.

If an entity classifed as A is sapient, it is allowed to apply for positions within the facility as "Personnel" and be treated as a full-fledged staff member upon acceptance. A database file will be created for them same as any other staff member.

### Attributes

Entities of Class A typically exhibit the following attributes:
- Friendly and cooperative behavior
- Non-hostile towards personnel
- Willingness to assist with research or facility operations
- Full integration into facility staff with corresponding rights and privileges
- Intelligence and problem-solving capabilities

### Rights
- Full rights and privileges as a staff member of the facility
- Allowed to exit the facility premises as needed
- Can interact freely with other personnel and entities within the facility

### Containment 
Class A entities are generally not subject to strict containment measures due to their cooperative nature. 
However, standard monitoring and security protocols apply to ensure the safety and order of the facility.


> In case of breaking facility rules or misconduct, Class A entities may be stripped of their rights and privileges as staff members and lose their right to become personnel within the facility entirely.
