## About the Project
Axion labs is my personal project, a fictional sci-fi universe that I have been working on for a while. It's a setting where all my characters and stories take place.

The main inspiration for it comes from Half-Life, ever since I played it as a kid, I have been amazed by the idea of an underground research facility, and exploring Black Mesa. Especially the chapter "Questionable Ethics" where you get to see the facility experimenting on alien creatures, that may not always be friendly. I wanted to create my own version of that, with my own characters and stories.

You could say its also inspired by the SCP Foundation, that came more of as a coincidence, as I stared to classify my creatures xD. Although I have to say, a big difference is that Axion is not a secret organization, and the public knows about it.

## What is this website?

This website is a collection of information about the Axion universe, including information about the characters, the facility, and the different planets that are explored. It is also a place where I can share my art and stories for the community, read up on the lore, and learn more about the universe I have created.

If you have any questions about the project, or want to reach out to me, please check out my [contact page](/Axion/contact) for more information.






## Explanation in Universe

Axion is a facility that explores different planets in space, where they study the environment, collect samples, and search for signs of life, magical artifacts, and anomalies.

Our mission is to expand our **understanding** of the universe and our place within it.
Try to communicate with alien species, and to learn more about the different planets and their inhabitants.

We are also dedicated to the study of magic in detail and its applications in technology, which is used in our research and development of new technologies.
